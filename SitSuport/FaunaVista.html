Sobre

FaunaVista é um aplicativo educativo sobre a fauna brasileira. Explore os biomas, conheça espécies ameaçadas de extinção, participe de expedições virtuais e construa sua coleção de espécies descobertas — todos os seus dados ficam armazenados no seu aparelho.

FAQ

Como registro um avistamento?

Durante as expedições virtuais, siga os objetivos e desafios apresentados pelo app para fazer registros fotográficos simulados das espécies que encontrar.

Como funcionam as missões e os pins?

As missões guiam sua exploração. Complete os objetivos para ganhar pins e acompanhar seu progresso pelos biomas.

Onde meus dados ficam salvos?

Todo o seu progresso é armazenado localmente no seu aparelho. Nada é enviado para servidores.

O app coleta meus dados?

Não. O FaunaVista não coleta, compartilha ou vende dados pessoais. Consulte nossa Privacy Policy na seção Legal abaixo.

O app usa minha câmera?

Não. Os registros fotográficos durante as expedições são simulações geradas pelo próprio app. O FaunaVista nunca acessa a câmera do dispositivo.

Como reporto um problema?

Envie um e-mail para faunavistastaff@gmail.com e responderemos o mais breve possível.

This Privacy Policy explains how the FaunaVista app handles information related to its users. FaunaVista is an educational and interactive app focused on exploring Brazilian fauna, allowing users to learn about endangered species, explore Brazilian biomes, and take part in virtual expeditions and discovery activities within the app.

1. Collection of personal data

FaunaVista was developed following the principle of data minimization and, at this time, does not require registration or account creation to use the app. The app does not directly request personal information such as: name; address; phone number; email address; ID documents; password; payment information. FaunaVista also does not have, in its current version, a user profile system or storage of personal information on its own servers.

2. Data stored on the device

To enable its features to work, FaunaVista may store information related to the user's progress locally on the device, such as:

  • discovered species;
  • biome exploration progress;
  • simulated expeditions completed within the app;
  • information related to expedition completion;
  • simulated photo records made during the expeditions.

This information is used exclusively to maintain the operation and progress of the app. Progress-related data is stored locally on the device and, in the current version, is not sent to FaunaVista's own servers.

3. Simulated photo records

FaunaVista features interactive virtual expeditions that simulate photographing Brazilian fauna species in natural environments. The photo records made during these virtual expeditions are simulations generated by the app itself and do not correspond to photographs captured by the device's camera. Therefore, FaunaVista does not request access to the device's camera and does not collect or store the user's personal photographs. The simulated records may be associated with expedition progress and the user's collection, remaining stored locally when necessary for the app to function.

4. Data and content from external services

FaunaVista uses external services to provide information and content related to the species presented in the app.

iNaturalist: used as a source of information related to fauna species and observations, and may provide data such as common name, scientific name, location, and other information made available by the platform.

Wikimedia Commons: used as a source of images of the species presented in the app. The images used are selected according to the licenses applicable to each file, respecting the conditions of use and attribution established by their respective authors and licensors.

FaunaVista does not claim ownership of content belonging to these sources. Information about the authorship, license, and origin of the content used can be found in the Sources & Credits section of the app.

5. Data sharing

FaunaVista does not sell users' personal data. In the current version, progress-related data stored locally is not shared with third parties by the app. If future versions begin to use services that involve the collection, storage, or sharing of personal data, this Privacy Policy will be updated to state which data will be handled, for what purposes, and with which third parties it may be shared.

6. Storage and deletion

Information related to the user's progress is stored locally on the device. Deleting the app may remove the local data associated with its operation, according to the behavior of the operating system and the device's storage settings. If FaunaVista ever offers data storage on servers or user accounts, specific mechanisms will be provided for requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of that data.

7. Privacy of children and teens

FaunaVista has an educational purpose and can be used by students. The app does not directly request personal information for account creation or user identification. Parents, guardians, and educational institutions may contact us if they have questions about how data is handled in the app.

8. Security

We adopt technical measures compatible with the app's structure to reduce risks related to unauthorized access to stored information. Since FaunaVista does not maintain, in its current version, a user account or its own database containing personal information, most progress-related information remains on the device used by the user.

9. User rights

Under applicable legislation, including the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD), users may exercise the applicable rights over their personal data, including those related to confirmation of processing, access, correction, and deletion, where applicable.

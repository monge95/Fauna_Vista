## 1. About FaunaVista

FaunaVista is an educational app that presents information about Brazilian wildlife, its biomes, and species threatened with extinction.

The app allows the user to:

- explore Brazilian biomes;
- learn about different species;
- consult information about animals and their environments;
- take part in interactive virtual expeditions;
- make simulated photographic records during virtual expeditions;
- track their exploration progress;
- build their collection of discovered species.

## 4. Expeditions and simulated records

FaunaVista has interactive expeditions that simulate the observation and photographic recording of Brazilian wildlife species within the app.

During these expeditions, the user may make simulated photographic records in accordance with the objectives and challenges presented by the app.

These records are part of the exploration experience and do not represent photographs taken by the device's camera.

FaunaVista does not use the device's camera to carry out these simulations.

Simulated records may be used to track the user's progress in expeditions, in their species collection, and in exploring the biomes.
